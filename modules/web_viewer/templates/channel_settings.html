Monitored channels

The channels this bot answers on. Stored in config.ini → [Channels] monitor_channels, and reloaded by the bot after every save. See the public channels page.

List of monitored channels

A hashtag channel starts with #: its key is derived from its name, and saving adds it on the radio when the node is not subscribed to it yet. A custom channel (no #) has to be created first on the Radio page, where you choose its key.

Channel slots on the radio Manage on the Radio page

The MeshCore node exposes a fixed number of channel slots ([Bot] max_channels). Saving puts missing hashtag channels onto free slots by itself; removing a slot is done on the Radio page.
