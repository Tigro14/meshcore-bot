Airtime

This is modelled airtime, not a measurement of the channel being busy. Over serial, BLE or TCP the bot is a client of a radio: it cannot see whether the channel was clear, only how long a packet of a given size occupies it at the current SF/BW/coding rate. Real occupancy (CAD, LBT) belongs to a radio driver, and a repeater's own occupancy comes from that repeater's API. The two shares are kept apart on purpose: rx is what this node hears — a local observation, not the airtime of the whole channel — and tx is what the bot spends itself.

A gap is not a zero: a period with no packet is absent from the result, so the line breaks there rather than dropping to 0. Percentiles are nearest-rank over the stored buckets, which is not the same measurement as the instant windows above.
